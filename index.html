Frequently Asked Questions

Is parking available at the hotel?

Please note that vehicle parking is currently not available on-site. Local street parking options nearby can be assisted by our staff upon arrival.

We are located in Patia, within 1.8 to 2.8 km of KIIT campuses and Rose Gardens. Biju Patnaik International Airport is approximately 11-12 km away (around 25-30 minutes drive).

What languages do staff speak?

Our friendly team is fluent in both English and Hindi to ensure seamless service for all guests.

Is Wi-Fi free for all guests?

Yes! High-speed Wi-Fi is available across all room types and common areas free of charge.
